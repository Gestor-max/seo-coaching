Google doesn’t want anyone to click through to a 404 error page and that’s why it keeps removing bad sites from its index. For a good rank your site should stay active 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

1. Not utilizing keywords effectively.

This is most likely 1 of the most critical area of site design. Choose the best keywords and possible customers will locate your web site. Use the wrong ones and your website will see little, if any, visitors.

2. Repeating the same keywords.

When you use the exact same keywords over and over again (known as keyword stacking) the search engines may possibly downgrade (or skip) the page or site.

three. Robbing pages from other websites.

How several times have you heard or read that “this is the internet and it’s ok” to steal icons and text from websites to use on your website. Do not do it. Several unethical website owners try to gain search engine visibility by using keywords that have nothing at all to do with their website. The keyword doesn’t have anything to do with the page topic. Nevertheless, since the keyword is well-liked, they believe this will boost their visibility. This method is considered spam by the search engines and might cause the page (or occasionally the whole website) to be removed from the search engine listing.

5. Keyword stuffing.

Somewhat like keyword stacking listed above, this means to assign numerous keywords to the description of a graphic or layer that appears on your website by utilizing the “alt=” HTML parameter. If the search engines find that this text does not actually describe the graphic or layer it’ll be regarded as spam.

6. Relying on hidden text.

You might be inclined to believe that in the event you can not see it, it does not hurt. Wrong…. Don’t try to hide your keywords or keyword phrases by making them invisible. As an example, some unethical designers my set the keywords to the same color as the background of the internet page; thereby, making it invisible.

7. Relying on tiny text.

This is an additional version of the item above (relying on hidden text). Do not attempt to hide your keywords or keyword phrases by making them tiny. Setting the text size of the keywords so small that it can barely be seen does this.

8. Assuming all search engines are the same.

Several people assume that each search engine plays by the exact same rules. This is not so. Each has their own rule base and is subject to change anytime they so desire. Make it a point to discover what every major search engine demands for high visibility.

9. Using free internet hosting.

Do not use free internet hosting if you are actually serious about increasing site visitors via search engine visibility. Many times the search engines will eliminate content from these totally free hosts.

10. Forgetting to check for missing web page elements.

Make sure to check every page within your site for completeness, like missing links, graphics, etc. You will find sites on the web that can do this for totally free.

This is just a couple of of the methods and techniques that you need to steer clear of. Don’t give in to the temptation that these techniques will work for you. They will do much more harm than excellent for your site.
